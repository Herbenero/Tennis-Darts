Tennis Darts

Alternate sides, lock a target, win service games, survive deuce, and take the set.

Setup Mode
Rally scoring

Serve, return, and break the board.

Each service game begins on the left side of the board: 5, 12, 9, 14, 11, 8, 16, 7, 19. The next point alternates to the right side: 1, 18, 4, 13, 6, 10, 15, 2, 17. Numbers 20 and 3 are the net and lose the point. The server’s first valid hit sets the Target Number. A double on the first dart is an Ace and instantly wins the point.

Active side Only the current side’s numbers are valid. Missing twice loses the point.
Target rally Establish a target number, then build high score until missing.
Tennis scoring Progress: 15 → 30 → 40 → Game. Deuce uses tiebreak rules.
